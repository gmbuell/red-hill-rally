Six from the same brush

The band is drawn fresh from its seed every time — edge wobble, ragged ends, dry-brush streaks, and stray flecks all vary a little, and never enough to be a different device. Pick a stroke to use its seed.

House rules (from the brand guide)
  • One band per piece, and a piece with a band skips the brush swipe.
  • Band text sets straight — the arch belongs to the ribbon banner.
  • A red band spends the composition's red allowance.
  • White bands live only on non-ice grounds where the edge can read — the tool enforces this.
  • Dry-brush streaks are painted in the ground color: regenerate the band if it moves to a different ground.
  • Keep the seed with the artwork — the same seed always redraws the same band.
