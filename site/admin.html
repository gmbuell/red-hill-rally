Mission Control

The Rally’s working numbers: the marquee standings, the shout-outs, and the shirt order.

Every gift

The book. One row per donation, newest first: who gave, what it counted toward, and which Rocket it credited. Search by name, amount or class. The download carries the gift id, which is the Stripe id, so it reconciles line by line against Stripe.

Put a gift on a Rocket

Naming a Rocket at checkout is optional, so a grandparent or an aunt can give without it — the gift counts for the school and for nobody’s class. This attaches the Rocket it was meant for, or the two or three it was meant for, and their dollars split the way they would have at checkout. Picking a gift that already names Rockets loads them, so this fixes a wrong one too.

Nothing about the money changes — the amount, the priority and the honor roll stay exactly as they are. Only the class and the Rocket it counts for.

Classrooms

For the marquee. Ranked by participation, the way the Rally Board ranks them; click any column to re-sort.

Rockets

For shout-outs. Every named Rocket under their class, biggest first; sort by raised for the school-wide list. A gift naming several kids splits its dollars, and each shirt’s share goes to its own Rocket.

Shirt order

For the printer. One row per Rocket, size and order, with the moment it came in, so a batch can be cut at a time as well as a day. Times are Pacific, the way the school counts them.

Change a shirt size

A family ordered a Youth Medium and the child needs a Youth Large. Pick the shirt and the size it should be. Nothing else moves: the gift, the shirt count, the classroom race and every total stay exactly as they are — only the size on the printer’s sheet changes.

The order keeps the time it came in, so the shirt stays in the batch it was always in. If that batch is already with the printer, tell them about the change too — this page can’t.

Record a check

A check left in the office, or cash at a Gathering — with a shirt on it if the family bought one. It counts exactly like a card gift: the ticker, the classroom race, the honor roll, the Rocket’s own total, and the printer’s sheet.

Put the whole amount they handed over in Amount received. A shirt’s cost comes off the campaign total here the same way it does on a card order, so a $20 cash shirt raises $10 and the rest pays for the shirt.

Credit a partner’s student

A business partner’s own child, counted as a participant for their class. No dollars: the partnership is already in the campaign total, and partner money stays out of the classroom race on purpose. This adds the participation and nothing else — no honor roll listing, no change to any total.

Fix a Rocket’s name

Donors type names by hand, so one child can land as “Audrey” and “Audrey Webber”. Each spelling counts as its own Rocket, which splits their total and credits the class with two participants instead of one. Fixing the name moves the gifts and corrects the count.

Typing a name already in that class merges the two, and their gifts and totals combine.

Teacher recaps

Each teacher’s own class: participation, dollars, their Rockets, and how far to the next prize. Nothing sends on its own. Open the recaps below and send the ones you want, when you want them.

Open every class as a printable recap, one class per page. Print or save the ones you want and send them yourself.

Opens in a new tab. Pick one class up top and print it to save that teacher’s page as a PDF, named for them. It names the students in each class, so a page goes to that class’s teacher and nobody else.
