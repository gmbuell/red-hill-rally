Why We Rally

One rally, six priorities. Where every Rally dollar goes

The Rocket Rally is how our community helps bridge the gap between basic district funding and the kind of educational experience we want for every Red Hill student.

This year, Rally fundraising is focused on six priorities that directly support students, enrich the school day, and improve our campus.

The six priorities

Here is what each one costs to run for a full year.

Essential Support Staff

$90,000

Red Hill PTA directly funds key personnel that district funding does not fully provide, including a full-time school counselor, a dedicated physical education teacher, and Tier II academic intervention specialists who support struggling readers and mathematicians before they fall behind.

The STEM Lab

$25,000

Led by All Things Science, our hands-on STEM Lab gives every student opportunities to experiment with coding, engineering, chemistry, circuits, and more. The goal is simple: build curiosity, confidence, and a love of science early.

Play With Purpose

$15,000

Through our partnership with Beyond Athletics, professional coaches lead organized games during lunch recess that encourage active, inclusive play. Students build teamwork skills, learn to resolve conflicts, and have more opportunities to feel included on the playground.

The Red Hill Garden

$15,000

Our garden gives students a hands-on way to learn about nutrition, environmental science, and the natural world. Funding supports garden maintenance, supplies, and a new grade-level-specific curriculum that enriches science learning across all grade levels.

Arts at Red Hill

$20,000+

PTA funding supports visual arts instruction through Art Masters, classical music education through Pacific Symphony’s Class Act program, Red Hill Performing Arts, and visiting educational assemblies. These programs give students meaningful opportunities to create, perform, listen, and experience the arts throughout their elementary years.

A Safer, Brighter Campus

$20,000–$40,000

This year, PTA is also investing in our physical campus. Planned improvements include classroom window tinting to enhance campus security, along with upgrades to the multi-purpose room used by students, staff, and families throughout the year. This one is a one-time investment rather than an annual cost.

So why is the goal $50,000?

You’ll notice those numbers add up to a lot more than $50,000. That’s on purpose.

The amounts above are what each program costs to run for a full year. The Rocket Rally is one push toward them, not the whole thing. Our $50,000 goal is the biggest single push we make all fall, and every dollar goes straight into this list.

One Rally. Six Priorities. One Red Hill.

Instead of treating these as separate needs, the Rocket Rally brings them together into one focused fundraising campaign.

Every donation helps fund the people, programs, and spaces that shape our students’ experience at Red Hill.

That’s why we rally.
