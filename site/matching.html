Double Your Gift

Your employer might match it.

Red Hill PTA is registered with Benevity and other matching platforms

Many employers match donations to nonprofit organizations, often doubling (or more!) the impact of your gift. Red Hill Elementary PTA is already registered with several common matching platforms, like Benevity, but every company’s process looks a little different. Here are the general steps to follow:

  1. Log in to your company’s HR or employee benefits portal

    Look for sections labeled “Giving,” “Charitable Contributions,” “Employee Giving,” or “Matching Gifts.”

    Some companies use third-party platforms like Benevity, YourCause, CyberGrants, or Bright Funds.

  2. Search for our organization

    Try “Red Hill Elementary PTA” or “Red Hill Elementary School PTA.” If your company uses Benevity, we should already appear as an eligible nonprofit.

    If you don’t see us listed, you may be able to add us manually using our Tax ID: 33-0973857.

    Still not there? Email fundraising@redhillpta.org and we’ll make sure we register with whatever platform your company uses.

  3. Submit your donation receipt

    Upload the email receipt Stripe sent when you gave — it shows the amount, the date, and our Tax ID, which is everything a matching request needs.

    Can’t find it? Email fundraising@redhillpta.org and we’ll resend a confirmation.

  4. Your company approves and sends the match

    Once approved, your company sends the matching funds directly to Red Hill Elementary PTA. Timing varies, but matches typically arrive within a few weeks to a few months.

Not sure where to start?

Log in to your HR portal and use the search bar for “matching gifts” or “charitable donations” — or reach out to your department’s HR representative.
