Rocket Rally partnership levels · September–October

Reach Red Hill Families During Rocket Rally

Red Hill Elementary serves roughly 510 students and their families in Tustin, North Tustin, and Santa Ana (ZIP codes 92705, 92780, 92782). The Rocket Rally is our biggest fall event — a school-wide fundraising campaign that begins with a community walk to school and rally on campus. A Rocket Rally Partnership puts your business in front of these families during our highest-energy week of the fall: on our digital marquee facing Red Hill Avenue, on our Rocket Rally webpage, across our social channels, and in our Rally newsletter issue.

Rally Friend

$250

  • Name on the Rocket Rally webpage
  • Social media thank-you during Rally week

Rally Supporter

$500

  • Digital marquee rotation (1 week, Rally week only)
  • Logo on the Rocket Rally webpage

Rally Champion

$750

  • Logo on the Rocket Rally event shirt
  • Featured Instagram post
  • Mention in the Rally newsletter issue

Rally MVP

$1,500

  • Instagram Story added alongside the featured post
  • Mid-size logo on the Rocket Rally walk-to-school sponsor boards (route and arrival gates)

Each tier includes all the benefits of the tiers above it. Partnerships are tax-deductible — Red Hill PTA is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 33-0973857.
