# **1. The 30-Second Elevator Pitch**

*Memorize the shape, not the words. Say it your way.*

*“Red Hill PTA does something most PTAs can’t: on top of field trips, arts, and a new weekly STEM Lab, we fund actual people — a school counselor, a PE teacher, and reading-and-math intervention support for every kid who needs it. None of that is guaranteed by district funding. It exists because families, neighbors, and local businesses fund it, about $300,000 a year, and every dollar stays at Red Hill. Would you like to be part of that?”*

The last line matters. Always end with an invitation, never with a trailing fact.

# **2. Core Talking Points**

* **We fund people, not just parties.** Counselor, PE teacher, and Tier II academic support — roughly $90,000 a year that touches every single student.
* **New this year:** a weekly hands-on STEM Lab for every grade, organized sports at lunch recess, safety upgrades including classroom window tinting, and garden improvements.
* **One fall ask, one spring party.** We consolidated our fundraisers into the Rocket Rally (October) and the spring Gala because families told us there were too many asks. Fewer, clearer, better.
* **Every gift counts, literally.** $25 buys STEM materials for a classroom; $250 funds a week of recess sports; $500 funds a week of STEM Lab; $2,500 funds a month of it.
* **Every dollar stays at Red Hill.** We’re a 501(c)(3); gifts are tax-deductible; and many employers double gifts through matching — always ask “where do you work?”
* **We report back.** Every donor gets our Spring Impact Report showing exactly what their money did. We don’t just take — we show.

# **3. Answers to the Questions You’ll Actually Get**

**“Doesn’t the district pay for all this?”**

Districts fund teachers and buildings. The counselor hours, PE teacher, intervention support, STEM Lab, recess sports, most field trips, arts, and assemblies at Red Hill are PTA-funded. If we stop, they stop.

**“I already paid for membership — isn’t that my donation?”**

Membership ($26ish) mostly covers state PTA dues and gives you a voice and the directory. It’s wonderful — and it funds almost none of the programs. The Rocket Rally and Gala are how programs get funded.

**“My kid is in 5th grade — why give when we’re leaving?”**

Two honest answers. First, your child benefits all this year — the counselor, STEM Lab, sports, and 5th-grade promotion are all funded by this year’s giving. Second, families before you funded your child’s kindergarten. A legacy gift pays it forward — and we’ll keep you posted on what it does.

**“Where exactly does my money go?”**

Ask for the one-pagers in this toolkit, or the short version: student support staff, STEM Lab, recess sports, campus safety upgrades, garden, arts, field trips, and community events. The full budget is public and was voted on by the association in May.

**“What happened to Rocket Launch?”**

It merged into the Rocket Rally — one unified fall campaign ending with a school-wide walk on October 7. Same purpose, way less confusion, no product sales, no door-to-door.

**“We can’t give much right now.”**

“Any amount genuinely helps, and so does your time — volunteers are how everything happens. Also: link your Ralphs card to Red Hill and part of every grocery run comes to the school. Costs you nothing.” Never pressure. Ever.

**“Is the PTA sitting on a pile of money?” (reserves question)**

We’ve built healthy reserves through years of strong fundraising — and this year the association voted to invest a planned portion of them into the STEM Lab, sports, and campus improvements. Even after that, reserves stay above best-practice levels. Deliberate investment, not deficit trouble.

# **4. How to Ask (Scripts)**

## **The two rules**

Rule one: **people give to people** — warm beats cold, always; open with your own connection to Red Hill. Rule two: **after you ask, stop talking**. Silence is the ask working. Whatever they answer — yes, no, maybe — thank them warmly. A “no” handled graciously often becomes next year’s “yes.”

## **Business drop-in / call script**

*“Hi, I’m \[name] — my kids go to Red Hill Elementary just up the street, and I volunteer with our PTA. We’re inviting a small group of local businesses to be Annual Partners this year: one decision, July to June, and you’re in front of hundreds of local families all year — including our new digital marquee on \[street]. Partnerships start at $250. Can I leave this one-pager and follow up with \[owner/manager] next week?”*

## **Parent-to-parent script (Rocket Rally)**

*“Have you seen what the Rally funds this year? The STEM Lab and the recess sports program my kid won’t stop talking about — plus the counselor and PE teacher. We’re trying to get every family to give something, any amount. Here’s \[student]’s link — and if your company does matching, it literally doubles.”*

## **Grandparent / neighbor script**

*“You don’t need a student at Red Hill to invest in it — a strong school lifts the whole neighborhood, including home values. Most neighbors give $50–$250 once a year, and every donor gets our spring report showing exactly what it did.”*

## **If they say yes**

Get it in motion immediately: take the gift online together, or write down the pledge, amount, and contact and hand it to the Treasurer that same day. Then the machine takes over — thank-you within 48 hours, receipt, impact report in spring. Your only remaining job: tell them thank you again next time you see them.
